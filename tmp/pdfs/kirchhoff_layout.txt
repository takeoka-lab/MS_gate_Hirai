                                                  The             Mølmer-Sørensen             gate             is             a             widely             used             entangling             gate             for             ion             platforms             with             inherent             robust-
                                          ness             to             trap             heating.             The             gate             performance             is             limited             by             coherent             errors,             arising             from             the             Lamb-Dicke
                                          (LD)            approximation            and            sideband            errors.            Here,            we            provide            explicit            analytical            formulas            for            errors            up            to
                                          fourth        order        in        the        LD        parameter,        by        using        the        Magnus        expansion        to        match        numerical        precision,        and        over-
                                          come             signiﬁcant             orders-of-magnitude             underestimation             of             errors             by             previous             theory             methods.             We             show
                                          that             fourth-order             Magnus             expansion             terms             are             unavoidable,             being             in             fact             leading             order             in             LD,             and             are
                                          therefore        critical        to        include        for        typical        experimental        ﬁdelity        ranges.        We        show        how        these        errors        can        be        dra-
                                          matically          reduced          compared          to          previous          theory          by          using          analytical          renormalization          of          the          drive          strength,
                                          by         calibration         of         the         Lamb-Dicke         parameter,         and         by         the         use         of         smooth         pulse         shaping.
